---

## Methodology

This list is curated from tango community consensus: milonga DJ practice worldwide, 
todotango.com historical records, tango.info metadata, and the NTTT database's own 
recognitionScore (which weights Rating + orchestraLevel + style + year). Songs are 
selected for their **milonga iconic-ness** — not just recording quality or historical 
importance, but actual dancefloor recognition. A dancer who has attended milongas for 
2+ years should recognize every Tier 1 song within 5 seconds.

**Why hardcoded over algorithmic:** The algorithmic tier 1 produced 468 songs but only 
15 overlapped with any curated list of 100 truly iconic milonga songs. The algorithm 
optimizes for database metadata quality, not dancefloor familiarity.

**Tier Definitions:**
- **Tier 1** — Absolute must-know. Played at virtually every milonga series worldwide. Opener/closer material.
- **Tier 2** — Essential for serious dancers. Played regularly at quality milongas.
- **Tier 3** — Known by dedicated milongueros. Deep cuts with strong identity.

## Recommendations

1. **Expand Pugliese catalog** — Only 127 songs for one of the Big 4. The missing iconic songs suggest incomplete import.
2. **Troilo gaps** — 253 songs is reasonable but El Marne and Una Canción are notable absences.

4. **Gardel** — Don't prioritize. His recordings are historically important but rarely played for social dancing.
5. **De Angelis** — At 230 songs it's well represented, but verify canonical versions of Cascabelito and El Cencerro.
