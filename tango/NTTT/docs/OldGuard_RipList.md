## Public Domain Timeline — How Far Back Can We Go?

**US Public Domain:** Recordings made before **1923** are fully public domain in the US.  
Recordings 1923–1946 entered public domain Jan 1, 2022 (MMA Act — 100 year rolling).  
As of 2026: everything recorded before **1926** is fully public domain in the US.  
**Argentine law:** 70 years post-publication. Most pre-1956 recordings are PD in Argentina.

**Practical answer for YouTube ripping:**  
You can go back to **~1907** — the very first tango recordings. The earliest usable  
dance recordings are ~1913-1917. Archive.org has many of these freely available.  
YouTube has restored 78rpm rips going back to 1910.

**Source priority:**
1. **Archive.org** — Fully legal, free, many restored 78rpm transfers
2. **YouTube "78rpm" channels** — Ivan Diaz, TangoTunes, El Tanguero channels
3. **Todotango.com** — Some streaming, not downloadable
4. **TangoTunes.com** — High-quality digital restorations (some free)
